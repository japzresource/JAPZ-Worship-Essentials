Channel EQ
Curve Shape
Drag a dot left / right to change its frequency, up / down to change its gain. B1-B8 switch that band on or off without losing its setting. Reset EQ puts every band back to flat/default. The Shape knob narrows or widens every band at once. The colored curve reacts to whatever is actually playing through this channel, colored by level like a console's RTA (green = quiet, red = hot); the white line is the exact combined EQ response.
Channel Sends
Sends an extra copy of just this channel to another output device at the same time — for a monitor mix, a separate recording interface, or a second speaker set. Independent of this channel's main Output routing.
