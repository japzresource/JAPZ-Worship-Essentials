What is Worship Essentials?
Worship Essentials is a live sound toolkit for worship teams and media crews. It puts ambient pads, one-tap sound effects, backing loops, full songs and a proper mixer into one screen, so a single person can run the atmosphere of a service from a laptop, tablet or phone. It runs entirely in your browser, and your sounds and settings are saved to your own workspace.
Sound PadsTwelve chromatic keys of ambient pads with seamless looping, smooth key-to-key transitions and an octave shift.
SoundboardEvery keyboard key is a pad. Assign any audio file to a key and trigger it with your keyboard. Hit Playlist, tap keys to build a timed playlist, and choose how it fades out.
LoopsBacking tracks with queues, trimming, fades, tempo and pitch control.
MixPer-channel EQ, compression, delay, reverb, panning, faders and extra output sends.

Channel EQ
Curve Shape
Drag a dot left / right to change its frequency, up / down to change its gain. B1-B8 switch that band on or off without losing its setting. Reset EQ puts every band back to flat/default. The Shape knob narrows or widens every band at once. The colored curve reacts to whatever is actually playing through this channel, colored by level like a console's RTA (green = quiet, red = hot); the white line is the exact combined EQ response.
Channel Sends
Sends an extra copy of just this channel to another output device at the same time — for a monitor mix, a separate recording interface, or a second speaker set. The channel fader doesn't touch it — only the volume slider on each send row below does — but Mute silences it along with the main channel.
